## The reason, in five sentences

> I would buy **IRCTC** at about **₹459** because:
> 1. The business is **the Railways' only online ticket seller, plus catering, water and tourism**, and I understand how it earns.
> 2. Its moat is **a government licence**, and it is **stable**, though the Railways can reprice it.
> 3. Management is **competent caretakers**, because **profit grew from ₹1,006 cr to ₹1,393 cr in three years with no debt, and they resisted the 2021 fee order**.
> 4. The price is about **0.80** of the three-year base-case value of ₹570, and the margin of safety comes from **buying at the lowest PE since listing, near the peer median**. Thin: the base case holds today's multiple, and over ten years it returns 9.96%, under the index fund.
> 5. If I am wrong, the downside is limited because **there is no debt and the licence cannot be competed away; the bad case is −17%**.
