# Textiles screen — 22 Sep 2026

**Audit: passed.** `report_audit.py` sampled 13 of 82 data points (15%, seed 42) on 2026-09-22; 12 matched exactly and one was a page read-time written a minute early, corrected in place.

## What was screened

- **Universe:** the 5 Nifty 500 companies NSE tags as "Textiles", from the official constituent file `ind_nifty500list.csv`. Four are yarn, fabric and home-textile makers who export most of what they make; one, Page Industries, is a branded innerwear licensee. All 5 were screened.
- **Data:** Screener.in consolidated pages read on 2026-09-22 at 02:21-02:22 IST. Page Industries has no subsidiaries and its Screener consolidated page is empty, so its standalone page is used. Prices are the NSE bhavcopy close of 21-Sep-2026. Second source for price, market cap, PE and yield: stockanalysis.com, same night. Promoter pledge from Trendlyne and web summaries of exchange filings, read the same night.
- **Full-cycle averages** are used for checks 1, 4 and 6 (ten years, FY2017-FY2026) and five-year sums for checks 2, 3 and 5. Textiles is not on the method's cyclical list, but cotton prices and export demand make it one, so single-year figures do not decide anything here.
- **Three stock splits corrected.** KPR Mill split 5-for-1 (ex-date 24 Sep 2021), Trident 10-for-1 (ex-date 13 Dec 2019), Vardhman Textiles 5-for-1 (ex-date 24 Mar 2022). The NSE year-end closes before those dates are unadjusted, so the ten-year PE and PB ranges below use Screener's split-adjusted EPS and book value against the close divided by the split factor for pre-split years. Page Industries and Welspun Living had no split in the period.
- **Welspun Living traded as WELSPUNIND** until its 2023 rename; its year-end closes for FY2017-FY2023 are read under the old symbol.
- **The sector event of FY26:** the United States put a 50% tariff on Indian textiles in two steps, 7 Aug 2025 and 27 Aug 2025. Welspun Living, with about 65% of revenue from the US, reported a Dec 2025 quarter profit of ₹3 crore against ₹123 crore a year earlier (Screener; Business Standard 12 Feb 2026). Trident's Dec 2025 quarter OPM fell to 9%. Both recovered in the Jun 2026 quarter; why was not checked here.
- **Left out:** nothing inside the Textiles tag. Other listed textile and apparel companies, such as Arvind, Gokaldas Exports, Indo Count and Alok Industries, are either below the Nifty 500 cut or tagged under another industry, and were not screened.

### How each check was computed

Same as the Construction Materials screen of the same date: ROE on average equity each year; FCF is Screener's Free Cash Flow row summed FY2022-FY2026; interest cover is (profit before tax + interest) / interest; OCF/PAT is five-year cash from operations over five-year net profit; dilution is the change in shares (equity capital / face value) from Mar 2021 to Mar 2026; check 8 is pledge plus the promoter stake from Sep 2023 to Jun 2026 on Screener's quarterly table.

## Short analysis — one section per survivor

### Page Industries (PAGEIND)

**The business in one line.** Page holds the exclusive licence to make and sell Jockey innerwear, loungewear and athleisure in India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal and the UAE, and Speedo swimwear in India; it sells through distributors, multi-brand retailers, exclusive brand outlets and online, and is paid on sale or short trade credit.

**Financial quality.** Sales grew from ₹2,129 crore (FY17) to ₹5,247 crore (FY26), 10.5% a year. Net profit grew from ₹266 crore to ₹764 crore, 12.4% a year. OPM has stayed between 18% and 22% for ten years through two cotton cycles; FY26 was 22%. ROE averaged 46.2% over ten years and was 52.5% in FY26, on a business that pays out most of what it earns (payout 87.6% in FY26, stockanalysis). Five-year cash from operations ₹3,403 crore against net profit ₹3,170 crore, a ratio of 1.07. Free cash flow was positive in four of five years (FY23 −₹165 crore, a capacity year). Borrowings ₹277 crore against equity ₹1,502 crore, D/E 0.18. The most important change of the last two years: growth came back. Sales fell 3% in FY24 (₹4,714 → ₹4,569 crore) and profit stalled at ₹569 crore; FY25 and FY26 then added ₹678 crore of sales and ₹195 crore of profit, with OPM back at 22-23% from 17-19%. The Jun 2026 quarter was ₹1,420 crore of sales, the highest on Screener's table.

**Moat.** Brand and pricing power, resting on a licence. The evidence is the return: 38-54% ROE every year for a decade with no debt, which no unbranded textile maker in India approaches. The licence is also the limit: the brand belongs to Jockey International, and Page earns it under an exclusive long-term arrangement whose renewal terms were not read here. Widening or narrowing: stable. Athleisure and online-first brands have taken share at the edges since FY22, and FY24's dip shows the brand is not immune, but the FY25-FY26 recovery says it has not been displaced.

**Top 3 risks.** (1) Licence dependence: the asset that earns 50% ROE is not owned; a change in royalty or scope would reprice the whole business. (2) Growth: 10.5% a year over ten years, 3% decline in FY24; at 54x earnings the price assumes the FY25-FY26 pace continues. (3) Promoters have sold 2.2 points of stake since Mar 2024 with no stated reason, on top of periodic sales since 2014; family succession is a question for a deep read.

**Valuation now.** PE 53.8 (own ten-year year-end range 46.4x-99.3x, median 69.1x; peer median 42x, but the peers are commodity manufacturers). PB 26.7 (own range 22.8-43.7, median 29.9). Dividend yield 1.52% (stockanalysis five-year range 0.69-2.16%). One word: **fair** against its own history; against anything else in this sector, expensive.

**Into the final three?** Yes. It is the only company of five to pass all five value checks, and it passes them by a wide margin on everything except price. The flag is the promoter selling.

## Sector verdict

Pass rate 1/5 after both steps (5/5 after the eight hard checks). Indian listed textiles is four commodity exporters, whose returns swing with cotton and US demand and whose margins the 50% US tariff of Aug 2025 showed to have no floor, plus one branded licensee that earns 50% on equity. Worth more time only for Page, and for KPR Mill at a lower price.
