**Why this screen exists.** The 23 Sep Power and Railways screens eliminated PFC, REC and
IRFC on step-1 checks 2 and 5, which fail every growing lender by design. The lender rule
decided on 23 Sep 2026 (`CLAUDE.md`, "Lenders are judged as lenders") replaces those
checks. This file re-runs the three names under that rule. Everything else from the
original screens stands: `power-20260923.md` and `railways-20260923.md`.

**Figures are standalone** unless marked, as the lender rule requires. Prices and PE:
Screener.in, read 2026-09-23 14:10 IST (PFC and REC consolidated pages, IRFC standalone).

**Moat stars are a judgement.** PFC and REC, 3 each: government ownership gives the
lowest cost of funds in power lending and together they hold about 40% of it (CRISIL),
but lending is a commodity product and about 76-85% of each loan book is to state power
utilities. IRFC, 4: it is the Ministry of Railways' dedicated financier, and its lease
agreements fix its margin at borrowing cost plus 0.35-0.40% and pass interest and currency
risk to the ministry (CRISIL). That protects the margin and also caps it.

**Provision cover estimate for PFC.** (gross − net) ÷ gross credit-impaired assets =
(1.11 − 0.15) ÷ 1.11 ≈ 86%. It is an estimate, not a reported figure.
