# Power screen — 23 Sep 2026

**Universe: the 13-name Power theme in `data/themes-in.json`** — all 13 in the Nifty 500. It is wider than the 17-name NSE "Power" industry in one way and narrower in another: it adds the two power lenders (PFC, REC) and leaves out smaller NSE-tagged names. A theme wins over an NSE industry of the same name, so the theme was used. All 13 were screened.

**No survivors.** Six names passed all eight hard checks. Five of them then passed 4 of 5 value checks and clearly failed the fifth, a case the keep rule does not cover; Tata Power passed only 3. This screen read the 4-of-5 case as a drop, the same reading used for CONCOR in the Railways screen. Under the other reading, those five come back as amber. See "Two readings" under step 2.

**Audit: passed.** `report_audit.py` sampled 30 data points (15%, seed 42) on 2026-09-23; all 30 re-read, 29 within 1% and 1 rounding (Torrent interest cover 4.6x against 4.55x).

## What was screened

- **Universe:** `india_data.py universe --theme "Power"`, 13 names.
- **Data:** Screener.in consolidated pages read 2026-09-23 from 03:46 IST. Prices: NSE bhavcopy 22-Sep-2026. Second source for price, market cap, PE and yield: stockanalysis.com via `crosscheck`, same night.
- **Own PE range:** Screener's price-to-earnings chart, weekly points, ten years.
- **Under 30 names**, so nobody was left out on size.
- **ROE bar (check 1):** **10%** for the eleven power producers, transmitters and distributors — power is on the asset-heavy list in `CLAUDE.md`. **8%** for PFC and REC, which are lenders, not power businesses. The choice does not change their result: PFC's ten-year ROE is 24.5% and REC's 19.1%.
- **Lenders:** PFC and REC. The method skips check 3 for lenders and nothing else. See the note under Eliminated.

### How each check was computed

Same as `defence-20260923.md`: ten-year averages for checks 1, 4 and 6 (FY2017-FY2026), five-year sums for checks 2 and 5 (FY2022-FY2026), FY2026 for check 3. Check 7 is equity capital FY2026 against FY2021, with bonus issues taken out where they moved the capital. Check 8: pledge from Trendlyne's shareholding page for the names that passed checks 1-7; promoter % Sep 2023 → Jun 2026 from Screener.
