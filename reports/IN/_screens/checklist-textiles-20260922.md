## Page Industries (PAGEIND)

### Gate 1 — can I understand this business? ★★★★ (4)

- **How it makes money, one sentence:** it makes Jockey innerwear, loungewear and athleisure in its own Karnataka plants and sells them through 1,16,606-plus retail outlets, 893-plus large-format stores and 1,579 franchised exclusive brand stores (AR), earning a branded markup on cotton garments.
- **What it sells in ten years:** the same categories under the same licence, which runs to 31 December 2040 (June 2018 extension; BusinessToday, Business Standard), plus Speedo swimwear.
- **Variables that decide whether it wins:** (1) the licence's continuity and cost; (2) volume growth in the premium innerwear and athleisure categories, where FY26 revenue grew 6.3%; (3) gross margin against cotton and competition from online-first brands.
- **Source:** the FY26 annual report, twelve years of Screener data, the 2018 licence coverage. Not a tip.

Four, not five: the business is simple, but the thing that earns 50% on equity is a contract, and its terms need reading.

### The Jockey licence — settled first, as instructed

|---|---|
| Term | Exclusive licence from Jockey International Inc., USA, for manufacture, distribution and marketing in India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, the UAE and the Maldives; Saudi Arabia, Bahrain and Kuwait added in FY25. Current term to 31 December 2040. Source: BusinessToday and Business Standard, June 2018; AR FY26 for the territories. The AR does not state the expiry year. |
| Renewal history | The relationship began in 1994 with Page's first operations. The June 2018 extension took the term to 2040; the expiry it replaced was not found in this pass. Jockey named Page "Licensee of the Decade" in 2026 for the second consecutive time (AR). |
| What Page pays | Not itemised. The AR reports foreign-exchange outflow "owing to royalty, import of raw materials, machinery, spares etc." of ₹6,773 million in FY26, one line for all of it. The royalty rate is not disclosed in the text searched. |
| What happens if it is not renewed | Page would lose the right to make and sell Jockey, which is nearly all of its revenue; Speedo is the only other brand and exports are under 1% of turnover (AR). There is no fallback brand. The mitigants are 14 years of remaining term, a 32-year relationship, two "Licensee of the Decade" awards, and Jockey's own choice to widen Page's territory in FY25. |

The licence outranks every margin in this file. It is not a reason to fail the gate today; it is the thing a deep read must confirm from the agreement itself, not from press coverage.
