## UltraTech Cement (ULTRACEMCO)

### Gate 1 — can I understand this business? ★★★★ (4)

- **How it makes money, one sentence:** it mines limestone, burns it into clinker, grinds that into cement, and sells bagged cement through dealers and bulk cement and ready-mix concrete to builders across India, earning the gap between the cement price and the cost of fuel, power, freight and limestone.
- **What it sells in ten years:** cement. More of it (the AR targets over 240 MTPA by FY2027-28 with ₹16,000 cr of committed investment), plus white cement, putty, ready-mix and building products around it.
- **The two or three variables that decide whether it wins:** (1) cement price minus cost per tonne, which the AR reports as EBITDA per tonne of ₹1,141 in FY26 (₹979 in FY25, ₹1,279 in FY22); (2) volume, which follows capacity and Indian construction demand; (3) what the acquired capacity earns — India Cements (14.45 MTPA) and Kesoram (10.75 MTPA) were bought in FY25 and India Cements was still loss-making at the time.
- **Source of what I know:** the FY26 annual report and twelve years of Screener numbers, not a tip.

Four stars, not five: the business is simple, but reading it needs regional pricing and cost-curve knowledge, and the acquisition returns are not yet visible.
