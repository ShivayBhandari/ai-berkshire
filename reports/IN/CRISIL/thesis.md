## The reason, in five sentences

> I would buy **CRISIL** at about **₹4,605.80** because:
> 1. The business is **India's leading credit rating agency plus a global research, data and benchmarking business for banks**, and I understand how it earns.
> 2. Its moat is **a SEBI-licensed rating name that investors prefer, plus surveillance fees for the life of each rating**, and it is **stable**; the research half is thinner.
> 3. Management is **steady and shareholder-friendly**, because **ten-year ROE is 30.8%, 57-72% of profit is paid out, there is no debt and the audits are clean**; but the parent sets the price of 13% of revenue.
> 4. The price is about **0.73** of the base-case value of ₹6,285, and the margin of safety comes from **a PE 16% below its own ten-year median and a net-cash balance sheet, not from the strict model**. Thin: the base case holds today's 38x multiple.
> 5. If I am wrong, the downside is limited because **there is no debt, ₹1,049 cr of net cash and a two-thirds owner; but the bad case is −24%**.
