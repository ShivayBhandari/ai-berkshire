## The reason, in five sentences

> I would buy **Indian Energy Exchange** at about **₹113.16** because:
> 1. The business is **India's main power exchange, earning about 4 paise on every unit traded, with 84% margins**, and I understand how it earns.
> 2. Its moat is **liquidity: 99.9% of day-ahead and real-time trading**, and it is **narrowing, by a draft rule of its own regulator**.
> 3. Management is **competent and modestly paid**, because **it built RTM and IGX and pays out 63% of profit**; but it lost the fight with CERC on timing, and a whistle-blower finding is undisclosed.
> 4. The price is about **1.02 times** the base-case value of ₹111.4, and the margin of safety comes from **nothing on the base case; only from coupling failing**. Cannot be completed as a discount.
> 5. If I am wrong, the downside is limited because **there is no debt, about ₹1,042 cr of own cash and a 3.09% dividend; but the bad case is −59%**.
