# Computer Age Management Services (CAMS) — thesis

**Written:** 2026-09-24, from `reports/IN/CAMS/research-20260924.md`
**Price on the report date:** ₹723.90 (NSE close 23-Sep-2026)

## The reason, in five sentences

> I would buy **CAMS** at about **₹724** because:
> 1. The business is **the back office for two-thirds of India's mutual fund money, paid mostly as a small share of the assets it services**, and I understand how it earns.
> 2. Its moat is **switching cost and scale in a two-firm market**, and it is **stable**: share of equity assets rising, total share slipping slowly, price per rupee falling by contract.
> 3. Management is **competent professional caretakers**, because **margins recovered from 43.7% to 46.4% within four quarters of a price cut, costs rose under 10%, and 65% of profit is paid out**.
> 4. The price is about **0.76** of the base-case value of ₹954, and the margin of safety comes from **profit growth, not from the multiple: 36.2x is near the bottom of its own range but three times the model's**. Thin.
> 5. If I am wrong, the downside is limited because **there is no debt, ₹790 cr of net cash and fees that recur every month; the bad case is −20%**.
