## The reason, in five sentences

> I would buy **UltraTech Cement** at about **₹11,155** because:
> 1. The business is **India's largest cement maker, selling 154 MT a year from every region**, and I understand how it earns.
> 2. Its moat is **national scale at a cost equal to the best regional producer**, and it is **widening**.
> 3. Management is **a strong executor with clean accounts**, because **200 MTPA arrived a year early, pledge is 0.0% and pay is under 1% of profit**; but acquisitions were dear and two cartel matters are open.
> 4. The price is about **0.96** of the base-case value of ₹11,570, and the margin of safety comes from **almost nothing on price; only the balance sheet and the share of a growing market**. Thin: at ₹9,980 it would be 0.86.
> 5. If I am wrong, the downside is limited because **net debt is under one year of EBITDA and FCF has been positive for ten years; but the bad case is −40%**.
